Operations function as an interdependent, continuously validated pipeline where thermal kill-steps, water activity thresholds and sanitary packaging govern finished product safety and shelf-life integrity.

Recruiter Perspective · Processing Operations

Academic grounding in food science combined with keen line discipline allows me to step immediately into shift operations, execute verified pilot blends, run standard CCP logs without supervision and maintain strict GMP compliance from startup to line clearance.

Quality assurance is a proactive protective discipline rather than passive end-line rejection. My focus integrates HACCP hazard analysis, strict SSOP sanitization, physical and chemical testing and audit-ready traceability records across every production shift.

Recruiter Perspective · Quality Assurance & Compliance

I bring formal academic HACCP/ISO grounding coupled with an unyielding eye for critical threshold limits. I am ready to conduct line inspections, manage lot quarantine tags without hesitation and keep documentation spotless for third-party food safety auditors.
